At the Resort

Start Right Here

Pioneer sits two miles from town and a boardwalk away from the Gulf, so most days start without anybody getting in a car. Here is what that looks like.

On the Beach

Our Own Boardwalk

The boardwalk crosses the dunes straight from the park onto the sand — no driving, no parking, no beach pass to buy.

On the Beach

Take the Cart Down

Rent a golf cart at the office and run the chairs, the cooler and the whole family down to the water in one trip.

Every Evening

Sunset From the Dunes

Guests drift out to the boardwalk most nights. It is the quietest, best seat on this stretch of the coast.

In the Park

Birds on the Pond

Egrets, herons and spoonbills work the pond at the edge of the park. Port Aransas sits on the Great Texas Coastal Birding Trail.

Event Weekends

The Sandbelt Race Track

Our own sandbelt track runs on event weekends — the crowd turns out along the rail and it is as good to watch as it is to race.

Getting Around

Catch the Town Shuttle

The resort shuttle runs into Port Aransas, so the truck can stay hooked up and nobody has to look for parking downtown.
