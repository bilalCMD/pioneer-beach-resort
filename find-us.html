Getting Here

Driving Directions

From San Antonio
  1. Take I-37 S
  2. Turn right on N Padre Island Dr/TX 358
  3. Continue on TX 358 as it becomes Park Rd 22 on Padre Island
  4. Park Rd 22 to TX 361
  5. Left on TX 361 to Gulfwind Drive
  6. Turn right on Gulfwind — Pioneer RV is on the right
From Houston
  1. Get on US-59 S
  2. Take I-69, US-59 S and TX-35 S to Oak Ln in San Patricio County
  3. Take TX-361 S to Aransas Pass – Port Aransas in Port Aransas
  4. Take the Aransas Pass – Port Aransas ferry
  5. Take TX-361 S to Gulfwind Dr
